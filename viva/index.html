An in-class conversation about what you know. Enter the code on the screen and your UMass email.

Before you start, please read this.

  • This conversation records when you leave this window (another app, another tab, another window) and for how long. Each time, the chat will tell you it noticed.
  • It also takes screenshots of your entire screen while you talk. When your browser asks, choose Entire Screen, not a window or a tab.
  • Pasting from outside this page is turned off.
  • Copying out of this page is turned off. Copy and paste still work inside it, for example from one line of your code to another.
  • Answer in your own words. There is a time limit: .
  • Some answers may be drawings: a drawing area opens below the chat. It works with a mouse, a pen or your finger.
  • Some answers may be code: a code area opens below the chat. If it starts with code, you can change it, and "Start over" puts the starting code back.
  • Some answers may be math: a math area opens below the chat. Type math the way you would say it, such as x^2, / for a fraction or sqrt; on a tablet a math keyboard opens.
  • If you get stuck, press "I'm stuck" for a small hint. Each hint costs some points on that part of the question, and you will see how many before you decide. You have .
  • With each answer, you say how sure you are, from 1 (guessing) to 5 (certain). It does not change your grade.
