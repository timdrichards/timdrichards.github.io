Container Simulator

Docker is hard to learn because you can't see what it does. Here you run the commands from COMPSCI 426, one guided step at a time, and watch a picture of your laptop change: the images you build, the containers you start, the ports that connect them, the data that survives.

  1. 1
    Pick an activity
    Each is one short task from a unit, 8 to 12 minutes.
  2. 2
    Follow the steps
    Press Run it, watch what changes, read what it means.
  3. 3
    Export and submit
    Your work saves in this browser; export the file for Canvas.

A teaching model, not real Docker: nothing you do here can break anything. Each activity ends with the same commands to try on your own machine.
