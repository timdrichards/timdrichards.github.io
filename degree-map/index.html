About this prototype. Built for the CICS advising conversation, November 2026. Course list, prerequisites and offering patterns come from the CICS BS in Computer Science requirements (2023 revision), the Spring and Fall 2026 course descriptions, and the course offering plan. Prerequisites are simplified: grade minimums, Informatics-major alternatives and outside courses are noted in each course's panel but not drawn. The math sequence is shown as students usually take it. It shows a selection of upper-level electives, not every course. Always confirm on SPIRE and with an advisor.
