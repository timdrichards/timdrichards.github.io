The degree at a glance

About this prototype. Built for the CICS advising conversation, November 2026. Requirements come from the CICS pages for the BS in CS (2023 revision), the BA in CS (Fall 2023 revision) and the BS in Informatics (Fall 2023 and later). Prerequisites and offering patterns come from the Spring and Fall 2026 course descriptions and the course offering plan. Prerequisites are simplified: grade minimums and alternatives that are not on the map are in each course's panel but not drawn. Each map shows a selection of electives, not every course. Always confirm on SPIRE and with an advisor.
