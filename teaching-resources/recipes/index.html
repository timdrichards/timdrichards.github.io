Teaching Recipes

One practice at a time, on one page, that you can use this week. Each recipe names the problem it solves, shows it working in a real course, gives the research behind it, and says where AI helps and where it does not.

A 20-minute session each week of the term, with the recipe posted the day before. 2 of 42 out so far; the rest show when they are coming.

Find recipes for your courseAnswer a few questions and get where to start. The researchEvery source, what it found, and the recipes that use it.

Four things every recipe does

  • Teaching first, AI where it helps. Each recipe is a sound practice on its own. Some need no AI at all.
  • Feedback throughout. Feedback on the work is one of the strongest influences on learning (Hattie and Timperley 2007), so every recipe says what feedback students get and how soon.
  • The idea behind it. One line and one reference from education research, so you can see why it works.
  • Time back to students. A recipe should take less of your time, not more, and say where the time saved goes.
