Recipe 32: When AI drafts the grading, what TAs do instead

This recipe is coming in Spring 2028, week 4. Its address will not change, so links to it will work when it is out.

What it will cover

Where AI helps
AI drafts the feedback (Recipe 2); TAs review it and move the hours saved to students
The idea behind it
Review has to be real, because TAs who over-relied on AI drafts passed on its errors (Ahmed et al. 2025); feedback only works when students take it up (Carless and Boud 2018)
