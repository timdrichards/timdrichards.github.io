Teaching Resources for TAs and TOs

Short, practical guides for graduate teaching assistants and teaching associates in computing: office hours, sections, grading, exams, assignments, and the job itself. Each page gives you defaults that work, scripts you can use, and the research behind them.

Written for TAs at the UMass Amherst Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences; the UMass-specific parts are marked, and the rest applies anywhere. New to TAing? Start with Your First Two Weeks as a TA. Last updated October 2026.
